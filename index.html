Censorship and Conformity in Ray Bradbury's Fahrenheit 451

themes connections about guestbook

This is my Honors English II project about themes in Fahrenheit 451 by Ray Bradbury and how those themes relate to real life. My project concerns how the themes censorship and conformity are portrayed in Fahrenheit 451, interprets what Bradbury's intent / message behind them were, and links them to in-world events.

Coded by hand with HTML and CSS by Jeanine Morgan. Inspired by old Geocities websites.
